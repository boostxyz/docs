# Boost

**Boost** (formerly RabbitHole) is onchain growth infrastructure that helps apps acquire and retain users through token incentives.

## Why Boost

For apps to generate sustainable onchain revenue, they need tools to acquire and retain users directly onchain—not through ads or offchain campaigns that don't translate to real engagement.

We also believe that if tokens represent ownership, there should be infrastructure to distribute that ownership to users based on the value they create. Boost makes it easy to reward users with tokens for completing valuable onchain actions, aligning incentives between protocols and their communities.

Boost has been building onchain incentive infrastructure since 2020, and the protocol has distributed millions in rewards across major L1s and L2s.
