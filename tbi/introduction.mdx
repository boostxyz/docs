# Time-Based Incentives

Time-Based Incentives (TBI) reward users for **how much they hold and how long they hold it**. Instead of paying out on a single qualifying action, a TBI campaign streams rewards every second a user's position is open and stops the moment they exit.

It's the right tool for any program that wants to reward sustained participation — deeper liquidity, longer holding periods, more committed users — rather than a single one-time interaction.

## How Rewards Work

The core idea is intentionally simple:

```
userReward = userBalance × holdDuration × rewardRate
```

At any moment, a campaign's per-second reward budget is split across every active position by share. Hold a larger position, earn a larger share. Hold longer, earn more. Exit, and your stream stops — but everything you've already accrued is still claimable.

Two properties worth knowing up front:

- **Join early, earn more.** Fewer participants in the early seconds of a campaign means a larger share of every drip.
- **No "use it or lose it."** Users can skip claims and pull everything they've accrued later in a single transaction.
